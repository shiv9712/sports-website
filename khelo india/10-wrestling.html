🤼 WRESTLING

Introduction

Wrestling is a combat sport in which competitors attempt to control their opponents using permitted techniques.

History

Wrestling is one of the oldest forms of organized competition and has been part of the Olympic tradition for a very long time.

Main Styles

Two major international wrestling styles are:

  • Freestyle
  • Greco-Roman

How Wrestling Is Played

Competitors wrestle on a mat and attempt to score through legal takedowns, control and other techniques.

Basic Rules

Wrestlers must remain within the competition rules for their particular style. Illegal techniques can result in penalties.

Equipment

  • Wrestling mat
  • Wrestling uniform
  • Appropriate protective equipment
  • Wrestling shoes

Important Skills

  • Strength
  • Balance
  • Agility
  • Technique
  • Endurance
  • Body control

Strategy

Wrestlers must understand their opponent's movements and use timing, positioning and technique to gain control.

Wrestling in India

Wrestling has a strong sporting tradition in India and is practiced in traditional training centers as well as modern sports academies.

Why Wrestling Is Popular

Wrestling combines strength, technique, discipline and tactical thinking.

Conclusion

Wrestling is a historic and highly technical sport that requires physical preparation, concentration and skill.
