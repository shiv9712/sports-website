🏹 ARCHERY

Introduction

Archery is a precision sport in which competitors use bows to shoot arrows toward targets.

History

Archery has been used historically for hunting and warfare, but it later developed into a recreational and competitive sport.

How Archery Is Played

Archers shoot a series of arrows toward a target. Their objective is to achieve the highest possible score through accurate shooting.

Target

The target contains several scoring areas, with shots closer to the center generally receiving higher scores.

Equipment

  • Bow
  • Arrows
  • Target
  • Arm protection
  • Finger protection
  • Quiver

Important Skills

  • Concentration
  • Accuracy
  • Consistency
  • Balance
  • Aiming
  • Controlled movement

Strategy

Archers must maintain a consistent technique for every shot. Mental focus is extremely important.

Archery in India

Archery is practiced in Indian schools, academies and competitive programs. India has developed successful archers at international level.

Why Archery Is Popular

It combines physical control with mental concentration and precision.

Conclusion

Archery is a sport where patience, consistency and accuracy are essential.
