🏓 TABLE TENNIS

Introduction

Table tennis is a fast racket sport played on a table divided by a net. It can be played in singles or doubles.

History

The sport developed as an indoor version of racket games and became internationally organized during the twentieth century.

How It Is Played

Players use small rackets to hit a lightweight ball across the table. The goal is to make the opponent unable to return the ball correctly.

Basic Rules

The ball must bounce according to the serving and rally rules. Players score points when their opponents make errors or fail to return the ball legally.

Scoring

Matches consist of games, with players attempting to reach the required number of points under competition rules.

Equipment

  • Table tennis table
  • Net
  • Table tennis ball
  • Racket

Important Skills

  • Serving
  • Spin
  • Footwork
  • Quick reactions
  • Ball placement
  • Control

Strategy

Players use different types of spin and placement to make returns difficult. Quick changes between attacking and defensive shots are important.

Table Tennis in India

Table tennis is played in schools, clubs and competitions throughout India.

Why It Is Popular

It requires extremely fast reactions and can be played in relatively small indoor spaces.

Conclusion

Table tennis is a technical and exciting sport that combines speed, precision, concentration and strategy.
