🏊 SWIMMING

Introduction

Swimming is an aquatic sport in which athletes race through water using different strokes.

Main Strokes

  • Freestyle
  • Backstroke
  • Breaststroke
  • Butterfly

How Swimming Is Played

Swimmers compete in marked lanes and attempt to complete a specified distance in the fastest time.

Individual Events

Swimming competitions include different distances and stroke combinations. Individual medley events combine multiple strokes.

Relay Events

Relay races involve several swimmers from the same team taking turns completing portions of the race.

Equipment

  • Swimming pool
  • Swimsuit
  • Goggles
  • Swim cap

Important Skills

  • Technique
  • Breathing control
  • Coordination
  • Endurance
  • Speed
  • Starts and turns

Swimming in India

Swimming is practiced across India in schools, clubs, training centers and competitive swimming programs.

Why Swimming Is Popular

Swimming is both a competitive sport and an important life skill. It develops coordination and endurance.

Conclusion

Swimming is a technical and physically demanding sport where small improvements in technique can make a major difference in performance.
