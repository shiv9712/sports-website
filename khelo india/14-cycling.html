🚴 CYCLING

Introduction

Cycling is a sport involving bicycles and includes several different competitive disciplines.

Major Disciplines

  • Road cycling
  • Track cycling
  • Mountain biking
  • BMX

How Cycling Is Played

Different cycling events test different abilities. Some emphasize endurance, some speed, and others technical bike control.

Road Cycling

Road events are conducted on roads and can involve long distances.

Track Cycling

Track events take place on specialized cycling tracks called velodromes.

BMX

BMX events involve smaller bicycles and may emphasize racing or technical movements depending on the discipline.

Equipment

  • Bicycle
  • Helmet
  • Sports clothing
  • Cycling shoes
  • Event-specific equipment

Important Skills

  • Balance
  • Speed
  • Endurance
  • Bike control
  • Tactical awareness

Cycling in India

Cycling is used both recreationally and competitively in India. Cycling competitions are organized at different levels.

Why Cycling Is Popular

Cycling provides opportunities for fitness, recreation, transportation and competitive sport.

Conclusion

Cycling is a diverse sport that can test endurance, speed, technical ability and tactical thinking.
