🏸 BADMINTON

Introduction

Badminton is a racket sport played using a racket and a shuttlecock. It can be played as singles, with one player on each side, or doubles, with two players on each side.

History

Modern badminton developed from earlier racket-and-shuttle games and became organized as a competitive sport in the United Kingdom. It later spread internationally and became one of the world's major racket sports.

How Badminton Is Played

Players stand on opposite sides of a net and hit the shuttlecock back and forth. The objective is to make the shuttle land inside the opponent's court or force the opponent to make an error.

Singles and Doubles

Singles: One player competes against another.

Doubles: Two players form a team on each side.

Basic Rules

A rally begins with a serve. Players must hit the shuttle over the net and keep it within the correct court boundaries. A player or team wins a rally when the opponent fails to make a legal return.

Scoring

Badminton uses rally scoring. A point is awarded after every rally, and games are played to a specified number of points under official competition rules.

Equipment

  • Badminton racket
  • Shuttlecock
  • Net
  • Badminton court
  • Sports shoes

Important Skills

  • Serving
  • Smashing
  • Drop shots
  • Clears
  • Net play
  • Footwork
  • Reaction speed

Strategy

Players use different shots to move their opponents around the court. They may combine powerful smashes with drops and clears to create opportunities.

Badminton in India

Badminton is extremely popular in India and is played in schools, clubs and professional competitions. India has produced internationally successful badminton players.

Why Badminton Is Popular

The sport requires speed, concentration, coordination and agility. It can also be played recreationally by people of different ages.

Conclusion

Badminton is a fast and technical sport that combines physical movement with strategy and precision.
