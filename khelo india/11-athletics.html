🏃 ATHLETICS

Introduction

Athletics is a collection of competitive track and field events involving running, jumping and throwing.

Main Events

Athletics includes:

  • Sprinting
  • Middle-distance running
  • Long-distance running
  • Hurdles
  • Relay races
  • Long jump
  • High jump
  • Shot put
  • Discus
  • Javelin
  • Combined events

How It Is Played

Different events have different objectives. Runners compete for the fastest time, while field athletes compete for the greatest distance or height.

Track Events

Track events are generally decided by time. Athletes attempt to complete their race as quickly as possible.

Field Events

Jumping events are measured by distance or height, while throwing events are measured by the distance of the throw.

Equipment

Equipment depends on the event and may include:

  • Starting blocks
  • Hurdles
  • Javelins
  • Discuses
  • Shot puts
  • Jumping equipment

Important Skills

  • Speed
  • Strength
  • Endurance
  • Coordination
  • Technique
  • Explosiveness

Athletics in India

Athletics is widely practiced in Indian schools, colleges, academies and national competitions.

Why Athletics Is Popular

It provides many different events and allows athletes with different physical and technical strengths to compete.

Conclusion

Athletics is one of the fundamental areas of international sport and forms a major part of major multi-sport competitions.
