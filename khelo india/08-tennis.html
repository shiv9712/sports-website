🎾 TENNIS

Introduction

Tennis is a racket sport played between two players in singles or two teams of two players in doubles.

History

Modern tennis developed from earlier racket games and became organized internationally during the nineteenth century.

How Tennis Is Played

Players hit a ball over a net into the opponent's court. The goal is to make the ball difficult to return while keeping shots within the permitted court area.

Singles and Doubles

Singles involve one player on each side, while doubles involve two players on each side.

Scoring

Tennis has a unique scoring system involving:

  • Points
  • Games
  • Sets
  • Matches

Basic Rules

The ball must land within the correct court area. Players serve from behind the baseline and alternate service according to the rules.

Equipment

  • Tennis racket
  • Tennis balls
  • Net
  • Tennis court
  • Sports shoes

Important Skills

  • Serve
  • Forehand
  • Backhand
  • Volley
  • Footwork
  • Accuracy
  • Ball control

Strategy

Players use different shot speeds, directions and spins to move their opponents and create openings.

Tennis in India

Tennis is played throughout India at school, club and professional levels. The country has participated in major international tennis competitions.

Why Tennis Is Popular

Tennis combines fitness, strategy, concentration and individual skill.

Conclusion

Tennis is a highly technical racket sport that rewards consistency, movement, accuracy and tactical thinking.
